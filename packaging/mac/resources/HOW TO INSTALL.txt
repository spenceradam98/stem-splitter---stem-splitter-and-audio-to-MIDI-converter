HOW TO INSTALL STEM-SCRIBE (Mac)

1. Double-click "stem-scribe-Installer.pkg".

2. If macOS says it "can't be opened because it is from an unidentified
   developer": close that message, then RIGHT-CLICK (or Control-click) the
   .pkg and choose "Open", then "Open" again.
   (This happens because this test build isn't signed with an Apple
   Developer certificate yet.)

3. Click through the installer. The separation model (about 80 MB) is
   always installed; you can untick the plugin formats you don't want.

4. Restart your DAW or rescan plugins. stem-scribe appears as an effect
   from YourBrand. Logic/GarageBand use the Audio Unit; most other hosts
   use the VST3.

5. The stem-scribe app is in Applications: drop a song on its window,
   wait for the analysis, then drag stems and MIDI out of it.

Splitting a 4-minute song takes a few minutes on Apple Silicon and longer
on older Intel Macs; results are cached, so reopening a session is quick.

Works on macOS 11 (Big Sur) or newer, on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.
