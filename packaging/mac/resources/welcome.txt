This installs stem-scribe, a stem splitter and audio-to-MIDI tool:

  - stem-scribe VST3        -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  - stem-scribe Audio Unit  -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  - stem-scribe app         -> /Applications
  - Separation model        -> /Library/Application Support/YourBrand/stem-scribe

After installing, restart your DAW (or rescan plugins) and look for
"stem-scribe" under the effects from YourBrand.
